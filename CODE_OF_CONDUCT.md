# Code of Conduct

RampLab is committed to a respectful, practical and inclusive learning environment for people across Africa and the wider Web3 security community.

Participants must:

- Be respectful across countries, languages, experience levels and professional backgrounds.
- Critique ideas and technical work without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive incident data.
- Keep security research within authorised, synthetic and local lab environments.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing and unwanted solicitation.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct undermines safety or inclusion. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers.
